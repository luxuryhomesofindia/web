Begin with the purpose of your home

A full-time residence, a multi-generational home and an occasionally occupied retreat can have different requirements.

A primary residence

Prioritise comfortable rooms, practical storage, kitchen workflow and the spaces your family uses throughout the week.

A multi-generational home

Discuss privacy, ground-floor accommodation, accessible circulation and connections between shared and independent areas.

An occasional-use residence

Consider how the property will be operated and maintained when unoccupied. Gardens, equipment and specialist amenities need realistic care arrangements.

Your intended use should guide the brief — not simply the size of the plot.

Understand the individual property

Before developing the design, establish the information available about:

  • Exact location and jurisdiction
  • Plot dimensions and boundaries
  • Access and road frontage
  • Orientation
  • Neighbouring buildings
  • Existing structures
  • Site levels and drainage
  • Ground conditions
  • Service connections
  • Applicable planning requirements

Do not assume that another property's design, foundation or approval route applies unchanged to yours.

Architecture that connects privacy and outdoor living

Outdoor spaces can become an important part of the home when they are planned for practical use. Explore:

  • Living areas connected to shaded seating
  • Courtyards with a clear purpose
  • Bedroom privacy
  • Gardens that suit your maintenance preferences
  • Terraces with appropriate access and drainage
  • Service areas separated from primary living spaces

Large openings should also account for shading, exposure, weather protection and privacy — not only the view.

Assess exposure where relevant

The property's actual conditions should inform materials and detailing. For exposed or coastal sites, review exterior finishes, metal components, fixtures and maintenance requirements with the design and engineering team.

Do not assume that every Pondicherry property needs the same protection system or foundation. Site investigation and professional assessment should establish the requirements.

Confirm the local approval process

The Puducherry Planning Authority provides building-permission information and an Online Building Permission System. The relevant authority, professional responsibilities and procedure still need to be confirmed for the actual property and proposal.

Your appointment should identify:

  • Who reviews planning requirements
  • Who prepares submission drawings
  • Which professionals are responsible
  • What approval coordination is included
  • Which charges are separate
  • What must be completed before execution

Avoid carrying Chennai-specific approval assumptions into this project.

Agree on regional delivery arrangements

For a project outside LHI's primary Chennai market, the delivery model needs explicit discussion. Ask about:

  • The assigned team
  • Local execution responsibilities
  • Consultant coordination
  • Site visits or site presence
  • Procurement and transport
  • Progress reporting
  • Homeowner approvals
  • Technical reviews
  • Completion requirements
  • Additional regional-delivery costs, where applicable

These details should be documented rather than left as verbal expectations.

Sunil Residence

Reviving Spaces with Elegance and Efficiency

Completed in 2023, the Sunil Residence renovation project in Redhills involved a comprehensive transformation of a 3,200 sq. ft home into a modern, functional and visually striking living space. Through strategic planning and premium upgrades, we reimagined the Residence to meet contemporary lifestyle needs while preserving its original structure.

Client:

Property Type:

The renovation included reworking spatial layouts, enhancing natural lighting, upgrading electrical and plumbing systems and introducing modern material palettes. Our end-to-end renovation approach ensured a smooth and efficient transformation—maximizing functionality without disrupting the home’s essence.

“Transforming the old into new—with vision, care and craftsmanship.”

Sunil Residence stands as a successful example of our renovation capabilities, combining design innovation with practical improvements. With complete transparency and in-house execution, we delivered a future-ready home tailored to the client’s evolving lifestyle.

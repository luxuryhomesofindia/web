Key Specifications & Structural Integrity

LHI recommends JSW Neosteel Fe 550D TMT or Tata Tiscon rebars and UltraTech 53 grade cement for main concrete works. Our onsite construction engineers supervise slump margins, moisture ratios, and execute regular concrete cube crush lab reports for structural safety verification.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does turnkey construction include?

LHI turnkey packages cover architectural design, soil testing, civil structure, electrical, plumbing, sanitary ware, flooring, painting, and basic elevation work.

What are the standard packages for house construction in Chennai?

LHI offers three construction packages: Basic at ₹2,250/sq.ft, Standard at ₹2,450/sq.ft, and Premium at ₹2,650/sq.ft, subject to specifications.

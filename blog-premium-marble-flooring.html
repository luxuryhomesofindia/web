Marble Flooring

Integrating premium Italian marble flooring villa designs is the ultimate way to establish luxurious character. From the bold veins of Statuario to the warm tones of Botticino, natural Italian marble provides a timeless material foundation for high-end custom homes.

Sourcing and Selecting Authentic Italian Marble

Italian marble is highly valued for its purity, durability, and fine grain. When LHI executes a premium villa project, our design team hand-selects marble blocks directly from leading importers, inspecting them for cracks and consistent veining.

Popular Marble Varieties for Custom Villas

  • Statuario Marble: Famous for its bright white base and bold, dark grey patterns. We arrange Statuario slabs in book-matched layouts to create dramatic, symmetrical wall features and floors.
  • Calacatta Gold: Featuring delicate golden-brown veins on a white background, this marble brings warmth and luxury to master bathrooms and dining halls.
  • Botticino Classical: A durable beige marble with light veins, excellent for high-traffic zones like hallways and staircases.

"A polished marble floor acts as a natural mirror, reflecting light beautifully and enhancing the spatial luxury of a custom home."

— LHI Principal Interior Designer

Meticulous Laying and Diamond Polishing

Installing Italian marble requires specialized skill. We apply a thick, level cement screed base to prevent cracking over time. Once laid with high-grade white adhesives, our team performs a multi-stage diamond grit polishing process, ending with a specialized protective sealer that guards the stone against stains while maintaining its deep gloss.

The Importance of Directional Alignment in Chennai

In Vastu Shastra, directional alignments dictate how solar energy and geomagnetic fields flow through a residential property. For a premium villa, ensuring the main entrance is located in the auspicious North, East, or North-East zones is crucial. This maximizes the intake of morning solar radiation, which brings positive energy and health.

Essential Vastu Layout Guidelines for Luxury Villas

  • The Master Bedroom (South-West): Positioned in the South-West zone (Nairuthi), the master suite represents stability and leadership. We design South-West rooms with thick structural walls to retain grounding energy.

  • Brahmasthan (Central Void): The center of the luxury home should remain light and clutter-free. LHI custom projects feature central double-height courtyards or glass domes to invite celestial energy down into the house.

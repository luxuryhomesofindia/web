What Is Included in a Construction Package? A Homeowner’s Guide

Published: October 2026 Luxury Homes of India Editorial 15 Min Read Chennai & Tamil Nadu
Package Specifications

15 Min Read
Updated October 2026

A house construction package generally combines agreed construction work, materials, labour and selected finishes under a defined scope and pricing structure. Depending on the agreement, it may cover foundation and structural work, masonry, plastering, doors and windows, flooring, plumbing, electrical installations, painting and handover.

Some providers also offer architectural design, structural engineering, interiors and turnkey project coordination. However, the package name and price per square foot do not tell you everything that is included. The decisive document is the written scope of work, supported by drawings, a material specification schedule, allowances, exclusions and payment terms.

For homeowners planning a house or luxury villa in Chennai, comparing these details before signing helps identify potential extra costs, clarify responsibilities and choose specifications that match the design, performance requirements and budget.

Executive Summary: Construction Packages Demystified

  • Scope Over Label: The package name (Basic, Standard, Premium) is only a commercial shorthand. The enforceable scope is determined by the written Bill of Quantities (BOQ), material brands, and unit allowances.
  • Material Allowances vs. Installed Cost: A ₹60/sq. ft. tile allowance covers the purchase price of the tile only; confirm whether mortar bedding, adhesive, skirting, cutting wastage, and grout labour are included.
  • Structural Specification Rigour: Primary materials (cement brand, Fe 550D TMT rebar, M-sand quality, concrete design mix) must follow approved structural engineering drawings regardless of the package tier.
  • Common Exclusions to Audit: Municipal approval scrutiny fees, soil test charges, compound walls, gate automation, modular kitchens, wardrobes, and utility connection deposits are frequently excluded from base civil packages.

1. What is a house construction package?

A construction package is an agreed collection of work, materials and services offered at a specified price or pricing basis.

A residential construction package may include:

  • Foundation and structural construction (excavation, footings, plinth beams, columns, and roof slabs).
  • Cement, reinforcement steel, aggregates and masonry materials (AAC blocks, wire-cut bricks, or fly-ash bricks).
  • Brickwork, plastering and related civil work (internal double-coat plastering, external weather-proof plastering).
  • Doors, windows, grills and handrails (main entrance door, bedroom doors, UPVC/aluminium windows, MS safety grills, SS railings).
  • Flooring, wall tiles and selected finishes (living room vitrified tiles or granite, bathroom anti-skid and dado tiles).
  • Plumbing, electrical installations and sanitaryware (CPVC/UPVC piping, modular switches, copper wiring, EWC, washbasins, CP fittings).
  • Interior and exterior painting (wall putty, primer, emulsion coats, weather-shield exterior paint).
  • Defined site supervision, inspections and handover activities (curing regimes, cube testing, punch-list remediation).

Crucial Takeaway: The exact scope varies by contract. Architectural design, structural engineering, statutory approvals, compound walls, landscaping, modular kitchens, wardrobes and specialist systems should be checked individually rather than assumed to be included.

The most important document is the scope of work. It should state what will be supplied, what will be installed, the applicable material specifications and allowances, who is responsible for each activity, and how variations will be priced.

How to Compare the Packages

  • Basic: Review whether the listed materials and allowances meet the project's technical requirements and your priorities. Suitable for rental properties, budget-conscious independent houses, or developments where high-end finishes will be installed post-handover.
  • Standard: Compare the actual upgrades against the additional price, including doors, windows, flooring, plumbing and electrical selections. Often represents the most balanced cost-to-performance ratio for family residences in suburban Chennai.
  • Premium: Check whether the higher allowances and customised options match the design. Bespoke joinery, imported finishes, automation and specialist equipment should still be confirmed separately before assuming complete luxury inclusion.

7. Elevation, roof finishing and external details

The supplied quotation describes the following exterior elevation treatments:

  • Basic: Architectural elevation with standard civil plaster finish.
  • Standard: Architectural elevation with exterior texture paint accents.
  • Premium: Architectural elevation with exterior wall tile or stone cladding finish.

These descriptions do not establish that all façade cladding, architectural lighting, pergolas, louvres, screens or specialist exterior features are included. Confirm the detailed elevation working drawings and material schedule before assuming full exterior execution.

Similarly, the listed weathering-course tiles do not, by themselves, establish the complete roof assembly. Confirm the applicable waterproofing layers (brick-bat coba or polymer membrane), slopes to rainwater downpipes, terrace drainage spouts, heat insulation requirements, and water ponding testing in the technical scope.

8. Does the package include labour and project management?

A material comparison alone cannot establish every commercial inclusion. A contract may specify premium cement and tiles but leave scaffolding, machine hire, or site cleanup to the owner.

Before signing, ask the provider to confirm responsibility in writing for:

  • Labour for civil, structural and finishing work: Skilled masons, carpenters, bar-benders, plumbers, and electricians.
  • Shuttering, scaffolding and equipment: Steel shuttering plates, props, scaffolding, concrete mixers, vibrators, and hoisting machinery.
  • Material transport, handling and storage: Unloading charges, on-site transit, covered cement godowns, and secure steel storage.
  • Site supervision and project coordination: Dedicated full-time site civil engineers and project management oversight.
  • Quality inspections and required testing: Concrete cube compressive strength tests, silt content tests, and moisture checks.
  • Site housekeeping and debris removal: Regular clearing of construction debris and final site cleaning before handover.
  • Construction water and electricity: Clarify whether the builder or owner provides water tanker supplies and temporary EB power.
  • Taxes, overheads and applicable charges: Confirm whether GST (typically 18% on construction contracts) is included in the quoted rate.
  • Defect correction and handover obligations: A defined Defect Liability Period (DLP) protocol covering remedial work.

LHI Service Framework: Luxury Homes of India presents turnkey construction, architectural design, structural engineering and interior execution among its residential services. Whether each service is included in a particular construction quotation must be confirmed in that proposal.

10. How to estimate and compare the construction cost

A per-square-foot rate is useful for initial budgeting, but it is not a substitute for a project-specific quotation.

For illustration only, applying the three supplied rates to a hypothetical 1,000 sq. ft. area gives the following arithmetic:

Package Tier Illustrative Calculation (1,000 sq. ft.) Indicative Base Civil Amount
Basic 1,000 sq. ft. × ₹2,250 ₹22,50,000
Standard 1,000 sq. ft. × ₹2,450 ₹24,50,000
Premium 1,000 sq. ft. × ₹2,650 ₹26,50,000

Disclaimer on Area Measurement: These are calculations using unverified quotation figures, not actual LHI project prices. The example assumes the entire 1,000 sq. ft. is chargeable at the stated rate; the actual measurement basis (e.g., covered porch, balcony at 70%, terrace at 50%) may differ.

Before relying on the estimate, confirm the chargeable area, included work, site conditions, exclusions, taxes and selected upgrades.

Checklist for Comparing Construction Proposals

  • Confirm the area measurement and pricing basis: Understand whether rates apply to carpet area, plinth area, or built-up area, and how balconies, staircases, and car porches are measured.
  • Obtain the complete drawings and specification schedule: Insist on Good for Construction (GFC) drawings rather than architectural concept sketches.
  • Compare equivalent scopes rather than package names alone: Align BOQ line items side-by-side to detect omitted items.
  • Record material allowances and upgrade charges: Clarify whether allowances include GST, transport, and cutting wastage.
  • Confirm labour, equipment, supervision and transport responsibilities: Ensure no surprise bills for crane hire or generator fuel.
  • Identify external works and interiors that need separate pricing: Add compound walls, gates, and modular cabinetry into your master budget.
  • Agree on payment milestones and variation approvals: Link payments to structural stages and require written change orders for cost additions.
  • Confirm the construction schedule and handover requirements: Establish a realistic critical path timeline with milestone penalties.

11. Frequently asked questions

What is included in a house construction package?

It may include structural and civil work, masonry, plastering, selected doors and windows, flooring, plumbing, electrical work, painting and specified finishes. The written agreement defines the actual scope.

What is the difference between Basic, Standard and Premium?

These labels usually describe different material selections, allowances and finish options. The supplied LHI quotation lists indicative rates of ₹2,250, ₹2,450 and ₹2,650 per sq. ft. Verify the current quotation before relying on these amounts.

Is architectural design included?

Do not assume so. LHI lists architectural design and planning as a service, but the supplied package comparison does not establish whether all design deliverables are included in each package.

Are labour charges included?

The material comparison alone does not settle every labour or management responsibility. Ask for a written scope that identifies labour, equipment, transport and supervision.

Are kitchen cabinets and wardrobes included?

Confirm them separately. Modular kitchens, wardrobes, custom furniture and decorative fittings should be identified explicitly in the quotation.

Are approval charges included?

Confirm who prepares and submits the documents and who pays statutory fees, deposits and other applicable charges.

Can I upgrade materials after selecting a package?

Any upgrade should be documented with the additional material and installation costs, availability, approvals and schedule implications before work proceeds.

Is Premium always best for a luxury villa?

No. The suitable option depends on the project design, technical requirements, selected finishes and budget. Compare actual specifications rather than relying on the package label.

How can I request a construction quotation from LHI?

Prepare the plot location, dimensions, approximate built-up area, available drawings, preferred specifications and required services. Request a project-specific proposal that clearly identifies the current rate, scope, exclusions and commercial terms.

Conclusion

A construction package is more than a per-square-foot figure. It defines the materials, work items, allowances and responsibilities agreed between the homeowner and construction provider.

The supplied LHI package comparison offers a starting point for reviewing selected specifications. Before choosing a package, align it with your drawings, engineering requirements, preferred finishes and complete project scope. Clear written inclusions, exclusions and variation terms help prevent misunderstandings during construction.
